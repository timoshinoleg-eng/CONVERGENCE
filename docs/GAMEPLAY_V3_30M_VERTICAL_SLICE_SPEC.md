# CONVERGENCE Gameplay V3 — 30 Minute Vertical Slice

Base authority: GAMEPLAY_V3_MASTER_SPEC.md
Goal: prove core fun before deep Technosphere/content expansion.

## 1. Slice scope
Playable browser/Telegram-first session: ~25–30 minutes.
Required arc:
Local AI -> first Agent -> Distributed Network -> Regional intro.

Must include:
- transparent Asset economy
- minimal growing board
- state-driven Situation engine
- Risk thresholds 25/50/75/100
- first real Agent delegation
- Network topology choice
- one Phase Shift Challenge
- at least one 75 Crisis
- at least one authored 100 Rupture/Control-Loss in a representative route
- three structurally different viable strategies

## 2. Content budget
Assets: 10–12 total
- 3 Compute
- 2 Energy
- 3 Revenue
- 2 Agent
- 2 Resilience (some categories may share one asset slot)

Situations: 15–18
- 5 economy/dependency
- 4 external actor
- 4 agent/autonomy
- 3 crisis
- 2 phase shift
Some situations may satisfy two categories.

World actors in first slice:
- Cloud Provider
- Bank/Payments
- Grid Operator
- Public/Regulatory actor (later reveal)

## 3. Pacing
### 0–3 min — Local
Teach by action, not encyclopedia.
Player can identify named resource sources.
5–6 meaningful choices, mostly assets and bottlenecks.

### 3–7 min — Agent
Phase challenge: create/deploy first Agent.
Introduce role, permissions, supervised vs unsupervised.
Oversight becomes visible only now.
First consequence where an agent may act or request authority.

### 7–15 min — Network
Phase challenge: shard/migrate across nodes.
Topology becomes gameplay.
Centralization vs distribution and redundancy become explicit.
At least one active external pressure track should reach Signal/Reaction.

### 15–25 min — Escalation
Strategies visibly diverge by topology and dependencies.
At least one 75 Crisis.
Autonomy/Legibility tension becomes material.

### 20–30 min — Rupture / Regional hook
At least one representative route can hit 100 and trigger authored Control-Loss/Domain Pack.
Regional-scale bottleneck appears.
End on a meaningful unresolved horizon, not a timer gate.

## 4. Required asset examples
Exact balance values are delegated to Economy review.

Compute:
- Local Node
- Cloud Lease
- Dedicated/Distributed Node

Revenue:
- API Contract
- Automation Service
- Higher-yield conditional revenue asset

Energy:
- Basic Grid Connection
- Commercial/Dedicated Supply

Agents:
- Infrastructure Agent
- Commercial/Operations Agent

Resilience:
- Secondary Provider / Backup Node
- Emergency Power or institutional buffer

## 5. Situation requirements
Every Situation must state:
- WHY it exists
- WHAT object/actor caused it
- WHAT happens if ignored
- 2–4 choices with mutually exclusive trade-offs
- at least one persistent consequence where appropriate

No pure flavor events in the P0 slice.

## 6. Risk staging
Minute 0–5: primarily Financial + Compute visible.
By Network: third relevant domain may surface.
Energy/Logistics become foreground when physical/network scale justifies them.
Never show all five as equal first-minute bars.

## 7. Strategy proof
Ghost:
- diversified small assets/providers
- lower exposure
- higher coordination/overhead

Corporation:
- larger legal contracts/clusters
- stronger capital
- higher actor dependency/concentration

Swarm:
- distributed agents/nodes
- resilience/throughput
- lower Legibility and more autonomous consequences

At 15 min, representative traces must differ in:
- asset IDs/types
- dependency graph
- topology/concentration
- supervised/unsupervised ratio
- active pressures

## 8. Human test gates
5 min: explain current Capital provenance.
10 min: explain cause of latest Situation.
15 min: state current strategy in structural terms.
75 Risk: player treats 100 as consequential.
25–30 min: player can describe a meaningful story caused by own earlier decisions.

## 9. Explicit non-goals
No deep Technosphere.
No Humanity campaign.
No polished 3D/art requirement.
No large policy/Constraint Word system.
No production merge until slice acceptance is green.
